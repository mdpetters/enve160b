Photochemical Cycle of NO2NO_2NO2​, NONONO, and O3O_3O3​

⏻ Learning Objectives

  • Perform photochemical reactions inside environmental chambers.

  • Explain the principle of operation of O3O_3O3​ and NOxNO_xNOx​ gas-phase monitors.

  • Explain the photochemical cycle of O3O_3O3​, NONONO, and NO2NO_2NO2​.

  • Measure and calculate the ozone produced at the photostationary state.

Background

Ozone is a gas composed of three atoms of oxygen (O3O_3O3​). Ozone in the air we breathe can harm our health, especially on hot sunny days when ozone can reach unhealthy levels. People at greatest risk of harm from breathing air containing ozone include people with asthma. Elevated exposures to ozone can affect sensitive vegetation and ecosystems, including forests, parks, wildlife refuges and wilderness areas. In particular, ozone can harm sensitive vegetation during the growing season. (Source: EPA). Tropospheric O3O_3O3​ is generated from to major classes of precursor molecules: volatile organic compounds (VOCs) and nitrogen oxides NOxNO_xNOx​, which is the sum of NONONO and NO2NO_2NO2​. Most of the direct emissions on NOxNO_xNOx​ are in the form of NONONO. Ozone levels exceeding 200 ppb are considered severe air pollution episodes. The current U.S. National Ambient Air Quality Standard for O3O_3O3​ is an 8-hour average of 80 ppb. Understanding VOC chemistry is critical to explain the total ambient O3O_3O3​ concentrations. Nevertheless, the photochemical cycle of NO2NO_2NO2​, NONONO, and O3O_3O3​ remains the starting point for modeling tropospheric O3O_3O3​ production.

Methods

This lab uses an environmental chamber, a photometric UV absorption O3O_3O3​ analyzer and a chemiluminescence NOxNO_xNOx​ analyzer.

The environmental chamber consists of a Teflon bag housed inside an enclosure that is lined with blacklights. The blacklights can be turned on/off using switches. An injection port is available to add reagents. You will receive a glass bulb that contains a nominal amount of NONONO. You will add the NONONO with the help of the TA and/or the instructor. Ozone is measured using a gas-analyzer. A 254 nm UV light signal is passed through the sample cell where it is absorbed in proportion to the amount of ozone present. Periodically, a switching valve alternates measurement between the sample stream and a sample that has been scrubbed of ozone. NOxNO_xNOx​ is measured using a second gas analyzer. The NOxNO_xNOx​ instrument determines the concentration of nitric oxide (NONONO), total nitrogen oxides (NOxNO_xNOx​) , the sum of NONONO and NO2NO_2NO2​) and nitrogen dioxide (NO2NO_2NO2​) in a sample stream. The principle of operation is chemiluminescence. Chemiluminescence is the emission of light from a chemical reaction and is triggered by the reaction of NONONO with ozone O3O_3O3​. The amount of light produced is linear with NONONO concentration. NO2NO_2NO2​ is measured by converting NO2NO_2NO2​ with to NONONO using heated molybdenum converter chip.
