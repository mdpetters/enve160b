Plagiarism

Please review the academic integrity section in the syllabus. Two important forms of academic misconduct relevant for the lab reports include

⚠ Misconduct

  • The use of generative AI software such as ChatGPT or GitHub Copilot is expressly forbidden in this course.

  • Verbatim copying or paraphrasing somebody else’s report, paper, web-based entry, book, or any other source without use of quotation marks and citation (verbatim text) or citation (paraphrasing).

Reporting Mechanics

This course will require you to turn in hand-written reports using either pen or pencil. Reports are due on Monday at the beginning of the lab, at the date outlined in they syllabus. You are required to use a single bound report book for all reports. Reports will be graded and returned the same week on Wednesday so you can prepare the next report.

The rubric may require either hand-drawn figures or computer-generated figures. Computer-generated figures must be printed and pasted into the notebook. You should leave at least 2 pages between reports for us to paste the rubric and leave space for comments. We are not prescriptive about the brand of notebook. However, it must state the course number, the quarter/year, your name and your student ID to identify you. A list of example notebooks you may want to consider is below. We do not endorse any of the vendor or products.

Report Grade

Each report has a specific rubric that outlines the required sections and components of the grade. In general the components are

  1. Prelab Summary

You will have to provide a hand-written prelab summary. The prelab summary is a sheet of paper with the date, the title of the lab, your name, your student ID, and a hand-written paragraph that summarizes the lab you are going to perform for a specific week. Your pre-lab summary will be collected at the beginning of the lab on Monday and also serves as a record attendance.

  1. Attendance Sheet

You will have to provide an attendance sheet for Wednesday labs. The attendance sheet should contain the date, the title of the lab, your name, and your student ID. It will be collected at the beginning of the lab on Wednesday.

  1. Report

You will provide a hand-written report that is submitted on Monday at the beginning of the lab session on the date indicated in the syllabus. Detailed guidance is provided for each report.
